How scores work and how far to trust them

Memory is derived from an estimated import footprint in MB (under 40 scores 5, under 80 scores 4, under 150 scores 3, under 300 scores 2, otherwise 1). Footprints are rough figures from memory, not benchmarks, so measure on your device before relying on them. Ease and Accuracy are 1 to 5 judgments (accuracy means numerical maturity and validation, not a guarantee for your model). Overall averages the three. Confidence (H/M/L) says how sure the entry is. Android status: Termux + Pydroid means it should install or ship there, Hard means compiled dependencies make it unreliable, No means not practical. Verify repos, versions and citations before citing anything.
